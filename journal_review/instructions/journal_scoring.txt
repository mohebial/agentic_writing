Reviewer recommendation scale — provide one of the four options per criterion
and as your overall recommendation:

  Accept           – Ready for publication; only trivial copyedits if any.
  Minor Revision   – Sound work with addressable concerns; no new experiments
                     required; can be re-evaluated by editor alone.
  Major Revision   – Requires substantial additional work, new experiments,
                     or fundamental re-analysis; must return to full review.
  Reject           – Fundamental flaws in novelty, rigor, or significance that
                     cannot be resolved by revision in this submission.

Confidence score — rate your confidence in your own assessment from 1 to 5:
  5 = Expert (this is your direct specialty)
  4 = Knowledgeable (closely related area)
  3 = Competent (familiar with the field)
  2 = Limited (adjacent field; some uncertainty)
  1 = Low (outside my expertise — rely on my critique with caution)

A reviewer's overall recommendation must be consistent with the weakest
individual criterion score. If any criterion is Reject, the overall
recommendation should generally be Reject.
