Foundation 5-point scoring scale (use for Overall Score and each criterion):
  5 = Exceptional  – Outstanding on this dimension; rare strength
  4 = Strong       – Clearly above average; minor gaps only
  3 = Adequate     – Meets minimum standards; notable but addressable gaps
  2 = Weak         – Significant concerns that undermine confidence
  1 = Poor         – Fundamental problems; not addressable without major revision

Higher is better. An overall score of 4–5 is in the fundable range.
An overall score of 3 may be fundable with conditions.
Scores of 1–2 indicate the application should be declined.
