Four core review criteria for private foundation grants (score each 1–5):

  1. Novelty & Innovation  – Is this a genuinely original idea? Does it challenge
                             existing approaches, open new directions, or take a
                             creative risk that conventional funding avoids?
                             This criterion carries the most weight.

  2. Feasibility           – Can the proposed work realistically be completed
                             within the proposed timeline and budget? Does the
                             team have the necessary expertise and track record?
                             Are risks acknowledged with contingency plans?

  3. Impact & Alignment    – What is the potential significance if successful?
                             Does it align with the foundation's stated priorities?
                             Is there a clear path from the project to meaningful
                             real-world or scientific impact?

  4. Budget & Sustainability – Is the budget justified and proportionate to the
                               scope? Is there a credible plan for the work to
                               continue or be sustained beyond the grant period?
