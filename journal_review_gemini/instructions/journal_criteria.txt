Four core review criteria for high-profile journal manuscripts:

  1. Novelty & Originality   – Is the central finding genuinely new? Does it
                               challenge prevailing models, open an unexplored
                               direction, or represent a conceptual advance beyond
                               incremental progress? The most important criterion.

  2. Technical Rigor         – Are the methods appropriate and rigorous?
                               Are statistical analyses valid and sufficient?
                               Are appropriate controls included?
                               Are findings reproducible and well-validated?

  3. Significance & Impact   – How important are the findings to the field?
                               Would this work change how researchers think or work?
                               Is the claimed significance supported by the data?

  4. Clarity & Presentation  – Is the manuscript clearly written and logically
                               structured? Are figures, tables, and supplementary
                               materials adequate and well-described? Is the target
                               audience able to follow the core argument?
